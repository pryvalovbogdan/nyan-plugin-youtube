# YouTube Introductory Video Script — Nyan Progress Bar

**Title**: Nyan Progress Bar — Full Feature Walkthrough & Website Sync Guide
**Target length**: ~5 min
**Format**: Screen recording with voiceover narration

---

## SCENE 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)

**On screen**: YouTube video playing. The default grey progress bar scrubber is visible.

> "This is your YouTube progress bar right now. Boring, right?"

**Cut**: Same video — but now a Nyan Cat GIF runs along a rainbow progress bar, with a glowing night-sky loaded buffer behind it.

> "Meet Nyan Progress Bar — a free Chrome extension that turns your YouTube scrubber into a running cat. Let me show you everything it can do."

---

## SCENE 2 — Install (0:20–0:45)

**On screen**: Chrome Web Store page for "Nyan Cat Extension".

> "Installing takes one click. Hit **Add to Chrome**, confirm the prompt…"

**On screen**: Confirmation dialog → extension added → extension icon appears in toolbar.

> "…and you're done. No account, no permissions to your data — everything runs locally."

---

## SCENE 3 — Automatic YouTube Refresh (0:45–1:00)

**On screen**: An already-open YouTube tab automatically reloads the moment the extension is installed.

> "If you already had a YouTube tab open, the extension refreshes it automatically — the cat appears immediately, no manual reload needed."

**On screen**: Progress bar now shows rainbow fill + night-sky buffer + a running cat at the scrubber position. Scrub the video to show the cat moving.

---

## SCENE 4 — What's on the Progress Bar (1:00–1:20)

**On screen**: Zoom in on the YouTube player bottom bar. Pause and scrub slowly.

> "The played portion gets a rainbow gradient. The loaded buffer gets an animated night sky. And at the playhead — your cat, running in place."

**On screen**: Hover over a video thumbnail on the home page to show the hover preview bar also gets the rainbow treatment.

> "Even the thumbnail hover preview bars on the homepage update — every progress bar on the site is covered."

---

## SCENE 5 — Opening the Popup (1:20–1:35)

**On screen**: Click the extension icon in the Chrome toolbar. The popup slides open.

> "Click the extension icon to open the popup. This is your control centre."

**On screen**: Annotated overview of the popup — title, theme toggle, language dropdown, cat grid, bottom buttons.

---

## SCENE 6 — Choosing a Cat (1:35–2:05)

**On screen**: The popup cat grid showing 12 animated GIF tiles.

> "There are 12 built-in cats to choose from."

**On screen**: Click "cute-kawaii", switch to the YouTube tab — the tall kawaii cat is now running on the bar. Switch back to popup.

> "Select any cat and it changes live on every open YouTube tab instantly."

**On screen**: Click through black cat → pixel cat → Garfield — each time cut to the YouTube tab showing the new cat.

---

## SCENE 7 — Custom GIF Upload (2:05–2:30)

**On screen**: Click the "+ Upload GIF" tile in the popup grid. File picker opens. Select a custom GIF from disk.

> "Don't like the built-ins? Upload any GIF file you want."

**On screen**: Custom cat appears in the grid as selected. The height and top-offset slider panel slides into view below the grid.

> "Once uploaded, a position panel appears so you can fine-tune exactly where your cat sits on the bar."

**On screen**: Drag the Height slider (8–80 px). Cut to YouTube tab — cat grows and shrinks in real time. Drag the Top Offset slider (−50 to +20 px) — cat moves up and down on the bar.

> "Height controls how tall the cat is. Top offset moves it up or down relative to the bar — useful since every GIF has different proportions."

---

## SCENE 8 — Language & Theme (2:30–2:50)

**On screen**: Click the language dropdown. Scroll through the list — EN, ES, PT, VI, ID, FR, TL, TR, PL, DE, UK.

> "The popup is fully translated into 11 languages and auto-detects your browser language."

**On screen**: Select "Українська" — all labels and button text switch to Ukrainian.

**On screen**: Switch back to English. Then click the theme toggle in the top-right corner. Popup switches from dark to light mode.

> "And there's a dark and light theme — it remembers your preference."

---

## SCENE 9 — YouTube Music (2:50–3:10)

**On screen**: Open a new tab to music.youtube.com. A song is playing.

> "The extension works on YouTube Music too. Same cat, same rainbow bar, all from the same popup."

**On screen**: Show the music player progress bar with cat + rainbow + night-sky. Scrub it.

---

## SCENE 10 — The Popup's Bottom Buttons (3:10–3:25)

**On screen**: Scroll popup to the bottom. Two rows of buttons are visible.

> "The bottom of the popup has quick links. Donate takes you to the support page, Customize opens the website customizer, More opens the homepage. And Rate Us…"

**On screen**: Hover over the Rate Us button — multicoloured hearts float up from the button.

> "…brings you to the Chrome Web Store reviews, with a little heart animation on hover."

---

## SCENE 11 — Website Introduction (3:25–3:40)

**On screen**: Open nyan-progressbar.com. The hero section loads with animated stats — 70K+ installs, 32K+ active users, 177 countries.

> "The companion website at nyan-progressbar.com is where the experience goes further. You can browse all cats, try the live preview, and sync changes directly to your open YouTube tabs."

---

## SCENE 12 — Website Customizer + Live Extension Sync (3:40–4:30)

**On screen**: Click "Customize" in the navbar. The customizer page opens. A mock YouTube player preview is visible on the right.

> "Head to the Customize page. The preview on the right shows exactly how the cat will look on a real video."

**On screen**: The page is split — left: cat gallery grid; right: live ScrubberPreview with rainbow bar and cat positioned at 42%.

**On screen**: Click "glitch-cat" in the website gallery.
- The preview on the right instantly updates.
- Cut briefly to an open YouTube tab in the background — the cat there also switches to glitch-cat.

> "Selecting a cat on the website sends the change live to the extension — your YouTube tab updates in real time."

**On screen**: Drag the Height slider on the website customizer panel. The preview cat resizes. Cut to YouTube tab — the cat on the real progress bar resizes in sync.

> "Adjusting the height or top offset on the website sliders also pushes those changes to the extension immediately."

**On screen**: Go back to the extension popup. The same cat is highlighted as selected — state is in sync.

> "And it works the other way too — switch a cat from the extension popup, and when you return to the website the gallery reflects your current selection. Everything stays in sync."

---

## SCENE 13 — Upload from Website (4:30–4:50)

**On screen**: On the customizer page, click the "+ Upload GIF" tile. Upload a custom GIF.

> "You can also upload a custom GIF from the website. The file is sent to the extension and immediately appears on YouTube — no need to open the popup at all."

---

## SCENE 14 — Other Pages (4:50–5:05)

**On screen**: Fast montage — click "Extension" nav link (feature cards page), then "Support" (donation tiles: Ko-Fi, Patreon, Crypto with QR code), then "Reviews" (star ratings + user review cards).

> "There's also a full feature overview page, a support page if you want to back the project, and a reviews page showing what users think."

---

## SCENE 15 — Outro & CTA (5:05–5:30)

**On screen**: Return to YouTube. A video plays with the cat happily running along the rainbow bar.

> "That's everything — 12 cats, custom GIF support, YouTube Music, 11 languages, and a live-synced website customizer, all for free."

**On screen**: Text card with links:
- Chrome Web Store (install link)
- nyan-progressbar.com
- Leave a review

> "Link to install is in the description. If it made your day a little more colourful, leaving a review on the Chrome Web Store helps a lot. Thanks for watching."
